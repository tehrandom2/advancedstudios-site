First launch

  1. Download the zip and open it to unpack the app.
  2. The build is unsigned, so a double-click is refused the first time. Instead, right-click (or Control-click) the app and choose Open, then Open again in the dialog. After that it opens normally.
  3. The first launch compiles shaders and takes about a minute; a “tuning the signal” screen shows meanwhile. Let it run; later launches are quick.

Why unsigned?

This build isn’t signed or notarised with Apple, so macOS asks once before it runs it; the right-click → Open step tells it you trust the app.
