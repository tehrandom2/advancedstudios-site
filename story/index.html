How to play

  1. Watch and listen. Each scene opens with a narrated moment. Click, tap or press Space to move on a line.
  2. Question the characters. When the narration stops, click a character (or their name tag) to talk. Pick a suggested question, type your own, or hold the mic button and speak. They answer in their own voice.
  3. Ask the right thing. Characters only know what they know, and they keep secrets until you ask the right question. Every secret you uncover goes in your Notebook.
  4. Find what the scene needs (the objective, top left) and the story continues. Stuck? Press Hint.

First visit: your browser warns about the certificate (it's this machine's own, made for the LAN). Choose Advanced → Proceed once; the microphone only works over this secure link. Typing always works if you'd rather not speak.

Episode 1: The Weight of Evidence

An experiment in stories that never have to end: little narrated scenes, characters who keep secrets, and you, questioning them by voice to work out what's really going on. Episode 1 is one hand-built slice of the idea. Where it goes from here is still being written.

A prize koi is missing. A round white cat is framed. A red cat on the wall knows more than she'll say. The board meets Friday.

How to play

  • Listen to each little scene unfold. Tap or press Space to move on.
  • Click a character to question them. Use a suggested question, type, or hold the mic and talk. They answer in their own voice.
  • They keep secrets. Ask the right thing and it goes in your Notebook. Learn what the scene needs and the story moves on.
  • Headphones on. First visit: accept the certificate warning once (Advanced → Proceed); the mic needs it. Typing always works.
  • Sound on, headphones if you have them. Allow the microphone when asked, or just type.
